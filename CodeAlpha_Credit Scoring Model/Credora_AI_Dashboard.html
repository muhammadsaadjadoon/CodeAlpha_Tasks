Objective & Approach

Turning raw financial history into a trustworthy risk signal

The brief: predict an individual's creditworthiness using past financial data. Credora AI treats this as a binary classification problem — creditworthy vs. not — solved with three interpretable, industry-standard algorithms.

Approach

Classification algorithms are trained on engineered financial features and benchmarked head-to-head so the strongest performer can be promoted to production scoring.

  • Logistic Regression — linear, fully explainable baseline
  • Decision Tree — rule-based, human-readable logic
  • Random Forest — ensembled trees for stability & accuracy
  • Feature engineering from raw financial history
  • Model assessment via Precision, Recall, F1-Score & ROC-AUC

Model Pipeline

From raw records to a risk decision

Five stages, in order, take an applicant from spreadsheet row to scored decision.

01

Data Collection

Income, debts, payment history and credit behavior are compiled per applicant.

02

Feature Engineering

Ratios like debt-to-income and income-to-loan are derived from raw fields.

03

Model Training

Logistic Regression, Decision Tree & Random Forest are fit on a stratified split.

04

Evaluation

Precision, Recall, F1 & ROC-AUC are compared across all three models.

05

Risk Scoring

The best model outputs a probability, mapped to a 300–850 risk score.
